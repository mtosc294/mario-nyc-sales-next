# SEO and AEO implementation plan

## Technical foundation

- Next.js App Router with static/server-rendered public pages.
- Route-specific metadata.
- Generated sitemap and robots rules.
- JSON-LD for WebSite, Person, RealEstateAgent and Article entities.
- Noindex for the private Opportunity Desk.
- Semantic HTML and crawlable internal links.

## Content clusters

### Seller cluster
- How much is my NYC apartment worth?
- How to sell a condo in NYC.
- How to sell a co-op in NYC.
- Seller closing costs.
- Sell occupied vs vacant.
- Renovate or sell as-is.
- Pricing with active competition.

### Buyer cluster
- Condo vs co-op.
- Buyer closing costs.
- Co-op board package.
- Financing and reserve requirements.
- Neighborhood pages by budget and property type.

### Investor cluster
- Small multifamily underwriting.
- Mixed-use risk review.
- Tax abatements and carrying costs.
- Rent regulation research workflow.
- Exit and liquidity planning.

## Answer-page standard

Every important page should include:
1. A direct, concise answer.
2. A clear definition.
3. NYC-specific steps.
4. Examples or calculations with assumptions labeled.
5. Common mistakes.
6. FAQ content only when visible on the page.
7. Author/reviewer and update date.
8. Primary-source citations.
9. A relevant service action.

## Measurement

Track separately:
- Non-branded impressions and clicks.
- Neighborhood and guide landing pages.
- Search-to-form conversion.
- Seller valuation starts and completions.
- Qualified consultation rate.
- Signed exclusive and closing attribution.
- AI/referral traffic where referrer data is available.
